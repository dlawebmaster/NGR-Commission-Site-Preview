Data will be used and displayed on a scholarship-search website built for students to find financial aid opportunities. The site lets a student enter their GPA, major, state, and education level, and returns matching scholarships from CareerOneStop's Scholarship Finder alongside a small set of independently-curated listings. Results are displayed publicly on the site's search/browse pages — showing scholarship name, amount, deadline, and a link to the sponsor's official application page. Data will not be sold, resold, or used for any purpose other than helping students discover scholarships they may be eligible for.
